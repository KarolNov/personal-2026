In 2024 I built an AI assistant that worked across Ceros products. It never got a public name: the project was sunset
after a change in leadership and strategy. What we learned building it carried into AI features across Ceros products,
Flex among them.

## What was in it

- **Voice to voice**, before realtime speech APIs were common: you talked to it and it talked back.
- **Artifact-style outputs.** Instead of answering only in chat, it produced things you could open and keep working on.
- **A visual guide** that let vision models point at exact spots on an image, so it could show you where to click
  instead of describing it.
- **A Python backend** (FastAPI, LangChain, Temporal, Pinecone) behind a React client.
